## Remaining discrepancies and likely cause
1. **completed cases (3 vs 16.9) and flow time (656 s vs 1018 s)** — the observed window is a ~53-min slice in which only 3 of 18 cases reach /hbw/store (12 stop mid-window without the terminal activity, 3 are right-censored; spec assumption: window truncation, not scrap). A full-horizon simulation of the same routing completes most cases, so the sim's completed count and flow time are structurally higher than the truncated observation. The flow-time gap is additionally inflated by the /sm/sort pooled fit (known outlier-driven mean ≈ 88.7 s vs median 12.1 s).
2. **overlapping steps/case (1.67 vs 0.91)** — the observed value counts overlaps across the 3 complete cases (which include the 3 concurrent pairs); the sim's per-case mean is lower because only the pairs present in each variant materialize and the spec's three documented pairs are variant-specific. Not a logic error — concurrency is implemented and verified (MR and sanity checks).
3. **hbw waiting (289 s vs 31 s)** — the spec attributes 64% of observed hbw waiting to "extraneous" hand-off/transport/release delays (resource idle while the case waits, mean 289 s) with no machine contention; no distribution for these delays exists in the spec, so the sim cannot reproduce them.
4. **vgr waiting (26 s vs 114 s)** — sim vgr_1 is the bottleneck (util ≈ 0.89, confirmed by MR1); the observed window had fewer in-flight cases, so contention was lower.
5. **release spacing median (2.0 s vs 4.6 s)** — phase-1 intra-burst IATs (median 0.90 s) are diluted by the 633 s transition gap and steady-state gaps in the pooled spacing; arrival EMD (287 s) is driven by the same transition-gap / burst-tail sampling (n=5 gaps, n=4 intra-burst IATs — wide uncertainty).
6. **variant JSD 0.085** — small-sample mix noise (9 variants, 18 observed cases); within expected range.

## Known flags (per binding constraints)
- **sm/sort mean inflation** — pooled triang retained as-is; 245.7 s outlier (WF_108_17) inflates the mean; no cap or slow-sort mode invented. Primary driver of the flow-time overestimate.
- **Sparse-sample distributions** — /dm/lower, /dm/cylindrical_drill, /mm/transport_from_to, /mm/drill, /pm/punch_gill, /pm/punch_recesses, /pm/punch_ribbing, /hbw/store, /ov/temper (n<5) use bounded empirical fallbacks (triangular/constant) as fitted; flagged in spec.
- **Per-resource splits** — /sm/transport, /wt/pick_up_and_transport, /vgr/pick_up_and_transport use the recorded per-resource splits (binding constraint 3); resources clearly differ (no overlap) and are not pooled.
- **Extraneous hbw waiting** — 64% of observed hbw waiting is unmodelled hand-off delay (no spec distribution); the sim reproduces only contention-based waiting.

## Assumptions made where the spec was ambiguous
- **Concurrency trigger** — the spec text said "as soon as the previous step's resource is released" but the spec's own evidence says the concurrent step is "requested while the previous step still holds its resource" with measured 26–39 s overlaps. The evidence (with actual overlap magnitudes) was followed: the concurrent step requests its resource at the previous step's service **start**.
- **Failed-attempt duration** — spec allows a short service; used 0.1× the sampled service time (deterministic rule, no new parameters).
- **Phase-2 continuation** — spec allows continuing the steady-state burst process beyond the 10 observed releases until the horizon; implemented that (draw bursts until `env.now ≥ horizon`), deterministic per seed.
- **Truncated retries** — if a case is still retrying at the horizon, the in-flight retry emits no row (only completed attempts are logged), consistent with "do not emit rows for steps not started".
- **Step probabilities** — all spec steps have probability 1.0; the skip-draw code path is present but never triggers.
